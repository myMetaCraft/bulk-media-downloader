How it works

  1. Copy the links

    In Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, tap Share and Copy link. Collect as many as you want, in a note or a chat.

  2. Paste them all at once

    Paste one link per line, or a whole message with links in it. The app finds every link and counts them.

  3. Press Download

    Pick the quality, or audio only as MP3. The files download side by side and land in one folder.

Questions

Is Bulk Media Downloader free?

Yes. The app is free, with no account, no ads and no limit on how often you use it. One batch can have up to 100 links.

Which sites does it support?

Instagram (Reels, video posts, photos and carousels), TikTok videos, and YouTube Shorts and videos. Many other video sites work too, because the app is built on yt-dlp.

Does it work on Mac or phones?

Not yet. It is a Windows app for Windows 10 and 11.

Why does Windows show a blue warning when I install it?

Windows shows that warning for new apps that are not yet widely downloaded. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Can I download private posts?

No. The app does not log in to your accounts, so it only downloads posts anyone can see without logging in.

Where do the files go?

Into your Downloads folder, under Bulk Media Downloader, with one folder per batch. You can pick another folder in the app.

Is it legal to download videos?

It depends on what you download and why. Downloading your own content, or content you have permission to use, is fine. Re-uploading other people's videos without permission is not. The sites' own terms may also limit downloading. You are responsible for what you download.
