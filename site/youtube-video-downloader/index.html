How to download YouTube videos in bulk

  1. Copy the links

    In YouTube, tap Share under a video, then Copy link. Links like youtu.be/… and youtube.com/watch?v=… both work.

  2. Paste them in the app

    Open Bulk Media Downloader and paste all the links at once. One per line, or a whole message: the app finds them.

  3. Download

    Choose the quality and press Download. Every file lands in one folder, and in one ZIP if you tick that box.

What you get

YouTube keeps the picture and the sound of high-quality videos apart; the app joins them into one MP4 file for you. "Up to 1080p" and "Up to 720p" prefer the most compatible format, which plays everywhere and opens in every video editor.

A link to a video inside a playlist downloads only that video.

Good to know

  • Age-restricted and members-only videos need a login and cannot be downloaded.
  • Long videos in 4K are large; check you have enough free space.
  • For Shorts, see the YouTube Shorts downloader.

Download only what you made or have permission to use. Posts that are private or need a login cannot be downloaded.
