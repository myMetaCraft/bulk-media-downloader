How to download TikTok in bulk

  1. Copy the links

    In TikTok, tap Share on a video, then Copy link. Short links like vm.tiktok.com work too.

  2. Paste them in the app

    Open Bulk Media Downloader and paste all the links at once. One per line, or a whole message: the app finds them.

  3. Download

    Choose the quality and press Download. Every file lands in one folder, and in one ZIP if you tick that box.

What you get

Each TikTok is saved as an MP4 file named after the creator and the caption. Several videos download side by side, so a list of 30 is done quickly. Choose Audio only (MP3) if you only need the sound.

Photo posts (slideshows) are saved as pictures when TikTok shows them without a login.

Good to know

  • Only public videos can be downloaded; the app never logs in to TikTok.
  • A video that was deleted or made private is marked with the reason, and the rest of the batch goes on.

Download only what you made or have permission to use. Posts that are private or need a login cannot be downloaded.
