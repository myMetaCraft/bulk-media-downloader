How to download Instagram photos in bulk

  1. Copy the links

    In Instagram, open a post, tap the paper plane or the three dots, then Copy link.

  2. Paste them in the app

    Open Bulk Media Downloader and paste all the links at once. One per line, or a whole message: the app finds them.

  3. Download

    Choose the quality and press Download. Every file lands in one folder, and in one ZIP if you tick that box.

What you get

Every photo is saved in the largest size Instagram offers. A carousel with several slides gives you one file per slide, numbered in order, and video slides inside a carousel are saved as MP4 next to the pictures.

Good to know

  • Only public posts can be downloaded; the app never logs in to Instagram.
  • Choosing Audio only (MP3) skips photos, so keep a video quality selected for photo posts.
  • For reels, see the Instagram Reels downloader.

Download only what you made or have permission to use. Posts that are private or need a login cannot be downloaded.
